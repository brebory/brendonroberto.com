Hypnos, from sketch to 3D

Hypnos is a character for Dreambeat Rush, one of my game projects. I worked him through from the inside out: a loose construction sketch to find the attitude, a pencil pass to settle the face and costume, then a turnaround to lock the design down: a nightcap, a star-covered robe, sandals and a big sack.

Then into 3D: modeling over the turnaround in Blender, blocking out the body, and sculpting the head.

I also designed Dreambeat Rush’s logo and a first pass at its main menu.

Rubbley concept art, 2020–21

Early sketches for Rubbley, from the winter I started the Unity prototype: his first faces, a space backdrop and notes on how the moves should read on screen.

Pixel art
